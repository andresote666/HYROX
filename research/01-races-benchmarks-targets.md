- **Wall ball height conflict:** fitnessvolt lists Open at 2.74 m for both genders (https://fitnessvolt.com/roxrace/weights/). Every other source gives 3.00 m for men, so treat 2.74 m as the outlier. HYROX's own race page does not state the height.
- **Weight changes for 2026/27:** none reported. Rundida's table, which cites the 26/27 Singles Rulebook, shows the same loads. I could not read the official PDF (https://maintain.hyrox.com/rulebooks/HYROX_RulebookSingles_EN.pdf) because it rendered as an image, so this is **not verified against the primary document**.

### Burpee Broad Jump rules
Per Rundida, citing Singles Rulebook 26/27 §8.4 (https://rundida.com/guide/hyrox-burpee-broad-jumps/):
- The chest must touch the ground, defined as "the nipple line making clear contact".
- Both feet take off and land together, with up to **5 cm stagger** allowed.
- The next rep starts from where you landed. No shuffling forward.
- Hands no more than **30 cm** in front of the toes.
- Feet may not land beyond the fingertips when coming up.
- Penalties: first infringement is a warning, then +15 s for each further infringement.
- **Conflict:** fitnessvolt says chest *and thighs* must touch. Findyouredge, citing 26/27, says chest only. Unresolved without the PDF.

### 2026/27 rule changes and clarifications
Source: Hybrid Fitness Media, 24 Jun 2026 (https://hybridfitnessmedia.com/2026/06/24/hyrox-competition-standards-2026-27-season/)
- Not completing any station in full is now a **disqualification**.
- Sandbag drops: **15 s penalty per drop**, no warnings. The two-drop automatic DQ is gone.
- BBJ: up to 5 cm foot stagger is now explicitly allowed.
- Sled Push: athletes must face the direction of travel.
- Lunges: the last rep counts when the lead foot first touches the ground beyond the finish line.
- Row: stay seated until the judge confirms the distance.
- SkiErg: jumping is allowed as long as the feet return to the base plate.
- Chalk: event chalk only, and you cannot carry it between stations. 2-minute penalty per infringement.
- Lingering in the start tunnel: 1-minute penalty.
- HYROX can declare an event "Out of Ranking".
- Doubles: partners must stay within 10 s of each other.

### 2025/26 changes still in force
Source: BarBend (https://barbend.com/news/hyrox-releases-new-season-rulebook/)
- Distance penalties were replaced by time penalties.
- No stepping onto the front line of the box on Sled Pull.
- Shoes may be removed for Wall Balls only, and must be carried across the finish.
- Starting in the wrong wave is a DQ.
- Spitting on the floor: 2-minute penalty.

### Academic literature
- **Brandt T, Ebel C, Lebahn C, Schmidt A. 2025.** "Acute physiological responses and performance determinants in Hyrox©…" *Front Physiol* 16:1519240, doi:10.3389/fphys.2025.1519240.
  - Sample: 11 recreational athletes, simulated race.
  - Completion 86.5 min, runs 51.2 min, stations 32.8 min.
  - Total time correlated with VO2max (ρ −0.71, p=0.01), body fat % (ρ 0.67, p=0.03) and endurance training volume (ρ −0.68, p=0.04).
  - Grip strength and body mass were not significant.
  - Wall balls produced the highest lactate and RPE.
  - Small sample, so preliminary.
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11994925/
- **Shi H, Zhong J, Xu J, Lu C. 2026.** "Structural screening and event-aware analysis of official HYROX split data…" *BMC Sports Sci Med Rehabil.*
  - 1,121 top-ranked records.
  - Run-split variability and late-race fade were **not clearly associated** with finish time. Results depended on the method used.
  - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13102-026-02187-6 (summary: https://bioengineer.org/study-finds-pacing-metrics-show-weak-link-to-hyrox-finish-times/)
- **"Performance Trends and Pacing Evolution in Elite HYROX©: Top 50 PRO Men 2018–2024."** *Applied Sciences* (MDPI) 16(13):6674.
  - Pacing went from clearly positive in early seasons to flatter recently.
  - I could not access the full text (403). https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/13/6674
- **Unconfirmed:** I could not find a "Davids 2025" HYROX study, which a Red Bull article mentions. A 2026 Preprints.org review (https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202607.1912/v1) was not read.

### Where the time is
1. **BBJ (−3:18).** At 10:03 he is about 4:30 behind the median. A 6:45 target is still about 1 minute slower than the median.
2. **Run 8 (−2:19).** His 8:19 is 2:36 slower than the average of his Runs 3–7 (5:43). The median Open man's Run 8 is only about 36 s slower than his mid-race runs. This looks like a breakdown after lunges (cramp or walking), not a fitness ceiling.
3. **Wall Balls (−1:49).** HyroxDataLab's 1:30 marker is 50+ unbroken reps.
4. **Sled Pull (−1:27) and Sled Push (−1:18).** Both are far off the medians (+1:40 and +2:00). This is unusual for a 97 kg athlete, so check Rio venue averages in ROXFIT Compare before assuming it is a strength problem.
5. **Roxzone (−1:01) and Lunges (−0:53).**

The top five items (items 1–4) account for about **10:11 of the 14:22**, roughly 70%.

**Already at or above median:** SkiErg, Row and Farmers Carry. Hold these; do not invest training time there.

### Running and body-composition notes
- His Runs 2–7 (5:16–6:15) are only modestly slower than the median.
- The run targets above ask for no more than about 5:15–5:40 between stations. This is consistent with HyroxDataLab's "about 55-minute 10k" readiness marker for 1:30.
- Brandt et al. found body fat % and VO2max related to finish and run time. That is consistent with, but does not prove the value of, his 97→94 kg goal (tiny study).

### Caveats
- Venue effects are not quantified: sled carpet friction and Roxzone length vary by venue.
- He has 16 weeks to cut 14 minutes. Lifting the stations to roughly median level on BBJ, wall balls, sleds and Run 8 is the main lever.
